About me

Before joining UGA Online, I supported learning technologies at UGA’s Center for Teaching and Learning and coordinated the Presentation Collaboratory Fellowship. I started my career as a high school band director, where I also wrote curriculum.

I’ve also worked with private clients in healthcare, real estate, and UI/UX education. Across all of it, I care about learning experiences that are engaging and usable, not just efficient.
